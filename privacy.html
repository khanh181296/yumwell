Yumwell – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-17_

Yumwell ("the app") helps you decide what to eat and cook. We built it to work without an account, and we keep as little data as possible.

What stays on your device

Your favorites, cooking history, weekly menu, shopping list, fridge selections, family members, diet choices, your own dishes and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not have a server and we cannot see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your calorie profile (optional)

If you fill in My calories, your sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal are used on your phone to estimate a daily calorie guide. This information is stored only on your phone, is never sent to us, to advertisers or to anyone else, and is not used for ads. You can change it at any time, and deleting the app deletes it.

The calorie guide and the per-dish calories are estimates for healthy adults. They are not medical advice and the app is not a medical device. If you are pregnant, have a health condition or an eating disorder, or are under 18, please talk to a doctor or dietitian.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general device information.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states that require it, the app

    • asks for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

  • On iOS the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not

    • personalized.

  • Yumwell Plus members do not see ads.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Weather suggestions (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Weather-based ideas, the app asks for your approximate location. The coordinates are rounded to about 10 km on your device and sent to MET Norway (https://api.met.no, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) only to get the current temperature. We do not store your location.

App analytics, crash reports and push notifications (Google Firebase)

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records app usage events (for example screens opened, a wheel spin, a recipe viewed) with an app instance ID, device model, OS version, language and approximate country. It does not record your name, email, body measurements or recipes.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits an error: device model, OS version, app version and the technical error.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a push token so we can send occasional news (for example new recipes). You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

Google processes this data on our behalf under its terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Dish photos

Dish photos are not stored inside the app. When you view a dish, the app downloads its photo from our website (khanh181296.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages) and keeps a copy on your phone. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the image. No account, profile or personal data is sent.

Notifications (optional)

Meal reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Crash reports and analytics

To fix bugs, release builds may send crash reports (Sentry) and anonymous usage events (Firebase Analytics) when these services are enabled. They do not include your name, email, recipes or lists.

Purchases

If you buy Yumwell Plus, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Yumwell is a general audience cooking app and is not directed at children under 13. Parents may enter a child's age, height and weight in My calories to see an estimate of the energy a growing child needs; for anyone under 18 the app never suggests losing or gaining weight. Like all profile data, it stays on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Change ad consent in Settings, or reset your advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Turn off weather and reminders in Settings at any time.
  • Delete everything the app stores at any time in Settings → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Under data protection laws such as the GDPR and Vietnam’s Personal Data Protection Law, you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because your data stays on your phone, you can do both yourself in the app; you can also contact us.
