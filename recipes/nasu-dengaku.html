🍆 Miso-Glazed Eggplant

Soft, silky eggplant halves spread with a sweet miso glaze and grilled until bubbling.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Eggplant2
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Miso paste3 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Sesame seeds1 tsp
  • Spring onion1 stalks

Steps

  1. Halve the eggplants lengthwise and score the flesh in a crisscross pattern about 1 cm deep.
  2. Glaze: stir the miso, mirin, sugar and 1 tablespoon of water in a small pan over low heat until glossy.
  3. Brush the cut sides with oil and cook cut side down in a pan over medium heat for 5 minutes. Flip, cover and cook until very soft.
  4. Spread the glaze over the cut sides and grill under a hot oven grill until bubbling. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and sliced scallion.
