🐷 Tonkatsu (Crispy Pork Cutlet)

Thick pork loin in a crunchy panko crust, served with shredded cabbage and tonkatsu sauce.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Pork chop400 g
  • Saltto taste
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Flour1.5 tbsp
  • Egg1
  • Panko breadcrumbs40 g
  • Cooking oil500 ml
  • Cabbage160 g
  • Tonkatsu sauce2 tbsp

Steps

  1. Use two 2 cm thick pork loin steaks. Cut through the connective tissue between meat and fat every 2–3 cm so they stay flat, pound lightly and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Coat each piece in flour, then beaten egg, then panko, pressing the crumbs on well.
  3. Heat the oil to 170°C. Fry for 2 minutes, turn and fry for 1½–2 minutes, then turn again for about 1 minute until deep golden and cooked through.
  4. Drain on kitchen paper, rest for a minute and cut into 2 cm strips. Serve with plenty of shredded cabbage and tonkatsu sauce.
