🐮 Gyudon (Beef Bowl)

Thin beef and onion simmered in a sweet soy dashi broth over rice. A 20-minute classic.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Beef225 g
  • Onion0.5
  • Spring onion1 stalks
  • Dashi powder120 ml
  • Rice cooking wine2 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Soy sauce3 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Rice (cooked)2 cups

Steps

  1. Thinly slice the onion and spring onion. Put the beef in the freezer for 10 minutes, then cut it into 7–8 cm pieces.
  2. In a cold pan, stir together the dashi, cooking wine, mirin, soy sauce and sugar. Spread the onion in it and lay the beef on top.
  3. Cover and bring to a simmer over medium, then lower the heat and cook 3–4 minutes, skimming off any foam.
  4. Add the spring onion and cook 1 more minute. Spoon the beef and sauce over rice.
