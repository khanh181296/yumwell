🥘 Soybean Paste Stew (Doenjang Jjigae)

Everyday Korean stew of fermented soybean paste with tofu, zucchini, potato and a little pork.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Pork85 g
  • Tofu255 g
  • Zucchini0.5
  • Potato1
  • Onion0.25
  • Chili1
  • Spring onion1 stalks
  • Doenjang (soybean paste)2 tbsp
  • Korean chili flakes1 tsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Cooking oil1 tsp

Steps

  1. Cut the tofu and zucchini into 2.5 cm cubes, the potato into thin bite-size pieces, the onion and chili into thin slices and the pork into thin strips. Chop the scallion.
  2. Heat a little oil in a small pot and stir-fry the pork with the soybean paste and chili flakes over medium heat for 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add 480 ml water (or anchovy broth), stir to dissolve the paste, add the potato and boil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add the onion, minced garlic, tofu, zucchini and chili and boil for 5–6 minutes more, until the potato is tender. Add the scallion for the last minute.
  5. Serve bubbling hot with rice.
