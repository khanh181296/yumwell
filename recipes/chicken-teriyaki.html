🍗 Chicken Teriyaki

Juicy pan-seared chicken thighs glazed in a glossy soy and mirin sauce. Ready in 20 minutes.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Chicken400 g
  • Salt0.13 tsp
  • Black pepper0.13 tsp
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Rice cooking wine2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Rice (cooked)2 cups

Steps

  1. Sauce: warm the soy sauce, mirin, cooking wine and sugar and stir until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Use boneless chicken thighs. Make shallow cuts every 1 cm on the smooth side, press them open so the thickness is even, then season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oil over medium-high and cook the chicken smooth side down for 5 minutes, pressing it with a spatula.
  4. Flip, lower the heat, cover and steam for 3 minutes. Wipe the excess fat out of the pan with paper towel.
  5. Turn the heat to medium, pour in the sauce and spoon it over the chicken until glazed and cooked through. Reduce the sauce 2–3 minutes more if you like it thicker.
  6. Slice and serve over rice with the sauce.
