🥣 Thai Rice Porridge (Jok)

Thick, creamy rice porridge with tender pork meatballs, ginger and crispy garlic, a Thai breakfast staple.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 50 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Uncooked rice150 g
  • Ground pork300 g
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Ginger30 g
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Fish sauce1 tbsp
  • Black pepper0.5 tsp
  • Egg4
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice and simmer it with 1.8 liters of water, stirring often, until the grains break down and it turns thick and creamy. Whisk it to make it smoother.
  2. Mix the pork with 1 tablespoon of soy sauce, the pepper and 1 minced garlic clove. Fry the rest of the garlic, minced, in the oil until golden, then lift it out.
  3. Drop small balls of the pork into the simmering porridge and cook until cooked through (71°C).
  4. Season with the remaining soy sauce and the fish sauce. Crack the eggs into the pot and cook until the whites are fully set.
  5. Serve topped with thin strips of ginger, scallions, herbs, the fried garlic and more pepper.
