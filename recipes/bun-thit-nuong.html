🍢 Lemongrass Pork Noodle Bowl (Bún Thịt Nướng)

Charred lemongrass pork over cool rice noodles with salad, herbs, peanuts and plenty of nước chấm.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 40 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Pork500 g
  • Lemongrass2 stalks
  • Garlic9 cloves
  • Lime4
  • Fish sauce7.5 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sugar8.5 tbsp
  • Cooking oil6 tbsp
  • Baking soda0.75 tsp
  • Vinegar3 tbsp
  • Chili2
  • Rice vermicelli200 g
  • Lettuce1 bunch
  • Cucumber1
  • Bean sprouts100 g
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Peanuts3 tbsp

Steps

  1. Cut the pork shoulder into 8 thin slices and pound them to about 5 mm thick.
  2. Marinade: mix the finely chopped lemongrass, 4 minced garlic cloves, 4 tablespoons lime juice, 3 tablespoons fish sauce, the soy sauce, 4 tablespoons sugar, 3 tablespoons oil and the baking soda (it will fizz). Coat the pork and marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour, or up to 24 hours.
  3. Nước chấm: stir the remaining 4½ tablespoons sugar and fish sauce with the vinegar, 3 tablespoons lime juice, 120 ml water, the rest of the garlic and the chili until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Soak the vermicelli in boiling water for about 5 minutes, rinse under cold water and drain well.
  5. Brush the lemongrass bits off the pork and throw away the used marinade. Heat a cast iron pan until smoking with 1½ tablespoons oil and cook half the pork for 1½ minutes on one side and 1 minute on the other, until cooked through. Repeat.
  6. Build bowls with noodles, lettuce, cucumber and bean sprouts, top with sliced pork, herbs and crushed peanuts, and pour over nước chấm.
