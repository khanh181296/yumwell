🥬 Caesar Salad

Crisp romaine with crunchy croutons, Parmesan and a creamy lemon garlic dressing made without raw egg.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • Bread4
  • Olive oil5 tbsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Mayonnaise1 cups
  • Lemon1
  • Dijon mustard2 tsp
  • Garlic1 cloves
  • Worcestershire sauce1 tsp
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Parmesan100 g
  • Lettuce450 g

Steps

  1. Croutons: heat the oven to 180°C. Cut the bread into cubes, toss with a drizzle of olive oil and salt, spread on a lined tray and bake for 10–15 minutes until golden and crisp. Let them cool.
  2. Dressing: whisk the mayonnaise, 60 ml olive oil, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, mustard, grated garlic, Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper until smooth. Stir in half the grated Parmesan, adding a teaspoon of water at a time if it is too thick.
  3. Put the chopped romaine in a big bowl with half the dressing, half the croutons and some Parmesan and toss. Add more dressing to taste.
  4. Top with the rest of the croutons, Parmesan and black pepper and serve.
