🍤 Chicken Pad Thai

Rice noodles tossed with chicken, egg, tofu and bean sprouts in a tangy tamarind sauce.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Flat rice noodles125 g
  • Tamarind paste1.5 tbsp
  • Sugar3 tbsp
  • Fish sauce2 tbsp
  • Oyster sauce1.5 tbsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Onion0.5
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Chicken150 g
  • Egg2
  • Bean sprouts150 g
  • Tofu0.5 cups
  • Garlic chives0.25 cups
  • Peanuts0.25 cups
  • Lime1

Steps

  1. Soak the dried rice noodles in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and rinse briefly under cold water. Use them within 10 minutes.
  2. Sauce: mix the tamarind puree, brown sugar, fish sauce and oyster sauce.
  3. Heat the oil on high. Fry the garlic and sliced onion for 30 seconds, add the thinly sliced chicken and cook about 1½ minutes.
  4. Push the chicken aside, scramble the eggs in the space, then mix together.
  5. Add the bean sprouts, tofu sticks, noodles and sauce. Toss gently about 1½ minutes until the noodles soak it up.
  6. Toss in the chives and half the chopped peanuts, then serve topped with the rest of the peanuts, lime wedges and chili if you like.
