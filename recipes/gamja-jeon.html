🥔 Korean Potato Pancakes

Crispy pancakes made from just grated potato and its own starch, served with a soy-vinegar dip.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Potato3
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Cooking oil4 tbsp
  • Chili1
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Vinegar1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Peel and finely grate the potatoes. Press them in a sieve over a bowl to squeeze out the liquid. Let the liquid settle, then pour off the water and keep the white starch at the bottom.
  2. Mix the grated potato with the starch, the salt and the thinly sliced chili.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat. Spoon in thin rounds and cook until golden and crisp on both sides, adding oil as needed.
  4. Stir the soy sauce, vinegar and 1 tablespoon of water together for dipping. Serve the pancakes hot.
