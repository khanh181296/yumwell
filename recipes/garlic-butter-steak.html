🥩 Garlic Butter Steak

Pan-seared ribeye basted with butter, garlic and thyme. Restaurant result at home.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Ribeye steak600 g
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Butter75 g
  • Thyme6 stalks
  • Garlic5 cloves

Steps

  1. Take the 2.5 cm-thick steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead and pat them dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan on high until smoking. Season the steaks generously and sear 2–2½ minutes until a crust forms, then flip.
  3. After 1 minute, push the steaks aside and add the butter, smashed garlic and thyme (careful, it spits). Spoon the melted butter over the steak for about 2 minutes for medium rare (52°C inside).
  4. Rest the steaks loosely covered with foil for 5–10 minutes, then drizzle with the pan butter.
