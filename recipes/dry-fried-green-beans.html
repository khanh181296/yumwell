🫛 Sichuan Dry-Fried Green Beans

Green beans blistered in a dry wok, then tossed with a little pork, garlic and chili.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Green beans450 g
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Ground pork60 g
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Dried chilies2
  • Rice cooking wine1 tsp
  • Soy sauce1.5 tsp
  • Sugarto taste
  • Sesame oil1 tsp

Steps

  1. Heat a dry wok over high heat until it just smokes. Add the beans and salt, turn the heat to medium-low and stir now and then until the beans are blistered and tender, about 20 minutes. Set aside.
  2. Heat the oil over medium-high heat and brown the pork. Add the diced garlic and seeded dried chilies and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Return the beans with the wine, soy sauce, a pinch of sugar and the sesame oil. Turn the heat to high, stir-fry for 1 minute and serve straight away.
