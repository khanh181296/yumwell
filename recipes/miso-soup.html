🥣 Miso Soup with Tofu

Light dashi broth with miso, silken tofu, wakame and spring onion. Ready in 15 minutes.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Dashi powder960 ml
  • Miso paste4 tbsp
  • Tofu200 g
  • Wakame seaweed1 tbsp
  • Spring onion1 stalks

Steps

  1. Make or heat the dashi (instant dashi powder is fine) until just about to boil, then turn off the heat.
  2. Put the miso in a ladle, lower it into the dashi and stir until fully dissolved. Allow about 1 tbsp miso per 240 ml.
  3. Add the tofu cut into 1.3 cm cubes.
  4. Just before serving, add the dried wakame and sliced spring onion. Do not let the soup boil after adding miso, or it loses its aroma.
