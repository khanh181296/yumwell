🦐 Garlic Butter Prawns

Golden seared prawns tossed in garlic, white wine, lemon and butter. Bring bread for the sauce.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 16 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Shrimp500 g
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Black pepper0.5 tsp
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Butter40 g
  • Dry white wine60 ml
  • Lemon1
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Bread4

Steps

  1. Toss the peeled prawns with 1 tablespoon olive oil and the pepper and leave for 20 minutes, no longer. Season with the salt just before cooking.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat and sear half the prawns for 45 seconds per side. Set aside and repeat with the rest.
  3. Return all the prawns, add the finely chopped garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the wine, scraping the pan, and let it mostly evaporate.
  5. Scatter in the butter and 1 tablespoon lemon juice and swirl until melted and the prawns are opaque and cooked through. Toss with chopped parsley and serve with lemon and bread.
