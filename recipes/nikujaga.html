🥔 Nikujaga (Meat and Potato Stew)

Thin beef, potato, onion and carrot simmered in a sweet soy and dashi broth. Japanese home cooking at its coziest.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Beef200 g
  • Potato600 g
  • Onion120 g
  • Carrot60 g
  • Shirataki noodles100 g
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Dashi powder350 ml
  • Rice cooking wine2 tbsp
  • Mirin0.5 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Green beans50 g

Steps

  1. Cut the potatoes into 3–4 cm chunks, slice the onion 1 cm wide and the carrot 5 mm thick, and cut the shirataki into 10 cm lengths. Cut large beef slices into bite-size pieces.
  2. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat and stir-fry the beef until it browns. Add the vegetables and shirataki and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add the dashi and bring to a boil, skimming off the foam. Add the sake, mirin and sugar.
  4. When it boils again, turn the heat to low, add the soy sauce and cover with a drop lid (or a sheet of baking paper). Simmer for about 10 minutes, until a skewer slides easily into the potato.
  5. Blanch the green beans, cut them into 3 cm pieces and scatter them over the stew. Serve hot.
