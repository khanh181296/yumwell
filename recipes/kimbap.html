🍙 Kimbap (Seaweed Rice Rolls)

Sesame-seasoned rice rolled in seaweed with beef, spinach, carrot, egg, fish cake and pickled radish.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 50 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Rice (cooked)4.5 cups
  • Sesame oil2.5 tbsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Beef225 g
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tsp
  • Sugar1.5 tsp
  • Garlic1 cloves
  • Spinach225 g
  • Carrot2
  • Pickled radish (danmuji)4
  • Egg2
  • Fish cake2
  • Nori seaweed4
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Cook the rice with a little less water than usual and keep it warm. Cut the beef into thin strips and mix with 2 teaspoons soy sauce, the rice wine, 1 teaspoon sugar, 1 teaspoon sesame oil and the minced garlic.
  2. Blanch the spinach, cool it in cold water, squeeze dry and season with 1 teaspoon sesame oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and cook into a thick omelet over medium-low heat, then cut into strips. Stir-fry the julienned carrot until just soft with a little salt.
  4. Cut the fish cake into strips and stir-fry with 1 teaspoon soy sauce, ½ teaspoon sugar and ½ teaspoon sesame oil for about 2 minutes. Stir-fry the beef over medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes until cooked through.
  5. While the rice is warm, fold in 1 tablespoon sesame oil and about ½ teaspoon salt.
  6. Spread about 1 cup of rice over each seaweed sheet, leaving 4 cm bare at the top. Line up the fillings, roll tightly and slice into 1 cm pieces.
