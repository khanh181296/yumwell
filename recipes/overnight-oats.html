🥣 Banana Overnight Oats

Creamy no-cook oats soaked overnight in milk with banana, honey and cinnamon. Breakfast is ready when you wake up.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 5 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats100 g
  • Milk250 ml
  • Banana1
  • Honey1 tbsp
  • Cinnamon stick0.25 tsp
  • Peanuts1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Mash half the banana in a jar or bowl and stir in the oats, milk, honey and cinnamon.
  2. Cover and keep in the fridge overnight, or at least 6 hours.
  3. In the morning, stir, add a splash of milk if it is too thick, and top with the rest of the banana and the crushed peanuts. Eat within 2 days.
