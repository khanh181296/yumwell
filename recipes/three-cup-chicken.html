🍗 Three Cup Chicken (San Bei Ji)

Chicken wings braised in sesame oil, rice wine and soy sauce with ginger, garlic and Thai basil.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 45 min🍽️ Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • Chicken wings1,000 g
  • Sesame oil1 tbsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Ginger6 slices
  • Garlic5 cloves
  • Dried chilies1
  • Rice cooking wine60 ml
  • Dark soy sauce3 tsp
  • Soy sauce1.5 tbsp
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Holy basil1 bunch
  • Spring onion2 stalks

Steps

  1. Warm the sesame oil, oil, ginger slices, sliced garlic and the halved dried chili in a wok over medium heat for about 2 minutes.
  2. Turn the heat to high, add the wings in a single layer and brown them on both sides.
  3. Add 60 ml warm water, the wine, both soy sauces and the sugar.
  4. Cover and cook over medium heat for 15–20 minutes, until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  5. Uncover, turn up the heat and stir until the sauce is thick and clings to the chicken. Toss in the basil and scallions until wilted and serve.
