🥚 Caramelized Pork and Eggs

Pork and boiled eggs braised in coconut water until glossy and tender.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 1 h 55 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Pork belly900 g
  • Egg6
  • Shallot1
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Fish sauce2 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sugar3 tbsp
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Black pepper1 tsp
  • Coconut water1,000 ml
  • Vinegar1 tbsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Spring onion2 stalks

Steps

  1. Boil the eggs for 9–10 minutes, cool them in ice water for 5 minutes and peel.
  2. Cut the pork into large cubes. Parboil 2–3 minutes in water with the vinegar and a spoon of salt, then rinse and pat dry.
  3. Make caramel: melt 2 tbsp sugar with 1 tbsp water in a small pan until deep amber, then carefully stir in 2 tbsp water.
  4. In a pot, fry the minced shallot and garlic in oil for 30 seconds. Add the fish sauce, soy sauce, remaining sugar, salt, pepper and the caramel, then the pork. Toss for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Pour in the coconut water, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently. Skim off any foam. Add the eggs after the first hour.
  6. Uncover and simmer 10–15 minutes to reduce the sauce. Top with spring onion and serve with rice.
