🍍 Sweet and Sour Pork

Crispy double-fried pork with peppers and pineapple in a bright sweet-sour sauce.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 50 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Pork400 g
  • Onion1.5
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Ginger15 g
  • Baking soda0.38 tsp
  • Cornstarch13 tbsp
  • Soy sauce3 tbsp
  • Cooking oil750 ml
  • Sugar70 g
  • Vinegar60 ml
  • Ketchup3 tbsp
  • Worcestershire sauce0.5 tsp
  • Oyster sauce1 tsp
  • Pineapple1 cups
  • Bell pepper1

Steps

  1. Cut the pork shoulder into 2 cm cubes. Mix with half a grated onion, 1 tsp each grated garlic and ginger, the baking soda, 2 tsp cornstarch and 2 tbsp soy sauce. Refrigerate overnight (18–24 hours) so it turns tender.
  2. Sauce: mix the sugar, vinegar, 3 tbsp pineapple juice, ketchup, Worcestershire, 1 tbsp soy sauce, oyster sauce, 4 tsp cornstarch and 120 ml water.
  3. Mix 5 tbsp cornstarch into the pork and rest 5 minutes, then roll each piece in the remaining cornstarch.
  4. Fry in oil at 180°C in batches for 3 minutes until golden. Drain on a rack.
  5. Heat the oil to 200°C and fry again for 1½ minutes until deep golden and crisp.
  6. In a wok with 1 tbsp oil, fry chopped garlic, ginger and onion cubes for 1½ minutes, add the bell pepper for 2 minutes.
  7. Add the sauce and pineapple, simmer about 2 minutes until thick, then toss the pork through for 10 seconds. Serve immediately so it stays crispy.
