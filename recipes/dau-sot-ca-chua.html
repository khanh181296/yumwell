🍅 Fried Tofu in Tomato Sauce

Golden fried tofu simmered in a sweet, tangy tomato sauce. A Vietnamese family favorite with rice.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 1 h🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Tofu450 g
  • Cooking oil500 ml
  • Tomato5
  • Tomato paste1 tbsp
  • Fish sauce0.5 tsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Spring onion1 stalks

Steps

  1. Cut the firm tofu into pieces, wrap in kitchen towels and weigh down with a plate for 20–30 minutes to press out the water.
  2. Heat the oil to 150°C and fry the tofu a few pieces at a time, stirring early so it does not stick, until golden. Drain on paper towels.
  3. Cut the tomatoes into bite-size pieces and put them in a pot with the tomato paste, fish sauce, salt, sugar and 120 ml water. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 10 minutes until saucy.
  4. Taste and adjust with more tomato paste, fish sauce, salt or sugar.
  5. Add the fried tofu and stir for a few minutes so it soaks up the sauce. Top with sliced scallion and serve with rice.
