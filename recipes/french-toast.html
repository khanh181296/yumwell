🍞 Classic French Toast

Bread soaked in egg and milk, pan-fried golden. A 15-minute breakfast with honey or fresh fruit.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Egg2
  • Milk125 ml
  • Bread4
  • Butter1 tsp
  • Honey1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs and milk in a shallow dish.
  2. Heat a large non-stick pan over medium heat with a little butter.
  3. Soak each slice of bread for about 10 seconds per side and let the excess drip off.
  4. Cook for 1½–2 minutes until golden underneath, flip and cook until the other side is golden and the center is set.
  5. Serve hot with honey, jam or fresh fruit.
