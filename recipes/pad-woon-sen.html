🍜 Thai Glass Noodle Stir-Fry (Pad Woon Sen)

Slippery glass noodles stir-fried with egg, shrimp, cabbage and tomato in a light, savory sauce.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato noodles100 g
  • Shrimp150 g
  • Egg2
  • Cabbage150 g
  • Tomato1
  • Onion0.5
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Oyster sauce1.5 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Spring onion2 stalks

Steps

  1. Soak the glass noodles in warm water until soft, drain and cut into shorter lengths. Slice the cabbage, tomato and onion, and mince the garlic.
  2. Heat the oil over high heat, fry the garlic for a few seconds, then add the shrimp and stir-fry until pink and opaque all the way through.
  3. Push to one side, crack in the eggs and scramble until fully set. Add the onion, cabbage and tomato and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles, oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar and 3 tablespoons of water. Toss until the noodles soak up the sauce, then add the scallions.
