🥓 Caramelized Pork Belly with Shallots

Thin slices of pork belly fried until the edges turn crisp and golden, then glazed with fish sauce caramel.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Pork belly500 g
  • Shallot4
  • Fish sauce2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Black pepper0.5 tsp
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Cooking oil1 tsp

Steps

  1. Slice the pork belly into thin pieces about 3 mm thick. Thinly slice the shallots.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat and add the pork in one layer. Let it render its fat, stirring now and then, until the edges are golden and crisp and the pork is cooked through.
  3. Pour off most of the fat, leaving about 1 tablespoon. Add the shallots and stir until fragrant.
  4. Add the sugar, let it melt and turn amber, then add the fish sauce and toss until the pork is glossy and the sauce has cooked down.
  5. Finish with black pepper and sliced scallions. Serve with rice.
