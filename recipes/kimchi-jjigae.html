🍲 Kimchi Stew

A bubbling, tangy stew of aged kimchi, pork belly and tofu. Korean comfort food.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Kimchi2 cups
  • Pork belly115 g
  • Korean chili flakes2 tsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Tofu170 g
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Soy sauceto taste

Steps

  1. Cut the kimchi and pork into bite-sized pieces, slice the tofu about 1 cm thick and chop the spring onion.
  2. In a pot with the oil, cook the kimchi, pork, chili flakes and minced garlic over medium-high heat until the kimchi softens and the pork is cooked.
  3. Add 120 ml kimchi juice if you have it and 500–600 ml water. Boil 5 minutes, then cover and boil on medium for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the tofu and spring onion, season with soy sauce or salt and pepper, and boil until the tofu is hot, about 5 minutes. Serve bubbling with rice.
