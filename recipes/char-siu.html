🍖 Char Siu (Chinese BBQ Pork)

Pork shoulder marinated in a sweet five-spice hoisin glaze and roasted until sticky and caramelized.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 1 h 15 min🍽️ Serves: 8

Ingredients

  • Pork1,360 g
  • Sugar50 g
  • Salt2 tsp
  • Five spice powder0.5 tsp
  • Black pepper0.25 tsp
  • Sesame oil0.5 tsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Hoisin sauce1 tbsp
  • Honey2.5 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves

Steps

  1. Cut the pork shoulder into strips 5–7 cm thick. Mix the sugar, salt, five spice, white pepper, sesame oil, wine, soy sauce, hoisin, 2 teaspoons honey and the minced garlic. Keep 2 tablespoons of this aside, rub the rest over the pork and marinate in the fridge for at least 8 hours.
  2. Heat the oven to 245°C with a rack in the upper third. Line a tray with foil, set a rack on it, pour 360 ml water into the tray and lay the pork on the rack.
  3. Roast for 10 minutes, then lower to 190°C and roast for 15 minutes more. Turn the pork, add a little water if the tray is dry, and roast another 15 minutes.
  4. Mix the reserved marinade with the rest of the honey and 1 tablespoon hot water. Brush it over both sides of the pork and roast for a final 10 minutes, until the pork reaches 63°C inside.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, brush with any remaining glaze and slice.
