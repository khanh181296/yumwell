🍳 Oyakodon (Chicken and Egg Bowl)

Chicken and onion simmered in a sweet dashi sauce, softly set with egg over rice.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Chicken280 g
  • Onion0.5
  • Egg3
  • Dashi powder120 ml
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tbsp
  • Spring onion1 stalks
  • Rice (cooked)2 cups

Steps

  1. Mix the dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sugar. Slice the onion 6 mm thick. Cut the chicken thighs into 2 cm pieces and sprinkle with the cooking wine.
  2. Crack the eggs into a bowl and just cut through the whites a few times with chopsticks. Do not beat them smooth.
  3. Spread the onion in a pan, pour in the sauce and bring to a simmer over medium. Add the chicken in an even layer.
  4. Simmer on medium-low, uncovered, about 5 minutes, flipping the chicken halfway, until cooked and the onion is soft.
  5. Drizzle two-thirds of the egg over the chicken. When it starts to set, add the rest and the spring onion. Cook until the egg is as soft or firm as you like.
  6. Slide it over bowls of hot rice with some of the sauce.
