🐟 Sweet and Sour Fish Soup

Southern Vietnamese soup: fish, tamarind, pineapple, tomato and bean sprouts.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • White fish fillet450 g
  • Fish sauce3 tbsp
  • Black pepper0.25 tsp
  • Tamarind paste85 g
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Okra170 g
  • Pineapple230 g
  • Tomato2
  • Bean sprouts170 g
  • Chicken stock1,400 ml
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Cut the fish into pieces and season with 1 tbsp fish sauce and a pinch of pepper while you prep.
  2. Mash the tamarind in 240 ml hot water for about 5 minutes, then strain. Slice the okra, cut the tomatoes into wedges and the pineapple into chunks.
  3. Fry the minced garlic in the oil until golden, then set aside.
  4. Bring the stock to a boil, add the fish and cook until it returns to a boil, about 3 minutes, then lower to medium.
  5. Stir in the sugar, remaining fish sauce and tamarind water. Add okra and pineapple for 2 minutes, then tomato for 1 minute, then bean sprouts for 1 minute.
  6. Taste for a sour-sweet-salty balance. Top with herbs, fried garlic and black pepper.
