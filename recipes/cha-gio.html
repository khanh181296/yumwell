🥢 Crispy Fried Spring Rolls (Chả Giò)

Rice paper rolls filled with pork, glass noodles, carrot and mushroom, fried twice until shatteringly crisp.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 2 h🍽️ Serves: 10

Ingredients

  • Mung bean glass noodles50 g
  • Ground pork450 g
  • Carrot2
  • Mushrooms30 g
  • Shallot30 g
  • Garlic1 cloves
  • Ginger5 g
  • Egg1
  • Fish sauce1 tbsp
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Black pepper0.25 tsp
  • Sugar3 tsp
  • Rice paper20
  • Cooking oil750 ml
  • Lettuce1 bunch
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Soak the glass noodles in warm water for 30 minutes, drain and cut into short pieces. Soak dried wood ear mushrooms until soft and chop finely.
  2. Mix the noodles, pork, grated carrot, mushroom, chopped shallot, garlic, grated ginger, egg white, fish sauce, 1 tablespoon oil, salt, pepper and 1 teaspoon sugar until evenly combined.
  3. Dissolve 2 teaspoons sugar in 250 ml warm water. Dip a rice paper for 5–10 seconds, put about 40 g filling near the bottom and roll up tightly, folding in the sides.
  4. Chill the rolls for at least 1 hour, then take them out 15 minutes before frying.
  5. Fry in batches of three at 170°C for 5–6 minutes until light golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack.
  6. Refry at 175°C for 1½–2 minutes until crisp. Serve with nước chấm, lettuce and herbs.
