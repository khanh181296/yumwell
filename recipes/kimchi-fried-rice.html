🍛 Kimchi Fried Rice

Leftover rice stir-fried with well-fermented kimchi and gochujang, topped with a fried egg.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Rice (cooked)2.5 cups
  • Kimchi0.75 cups
  • Bacon100 g
  • Onion0.25
  • Spring onion1 stalks
  • Carrot1
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Gochujang1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil0.5 tbsp
  • Egg2
  • Nori seaweed1
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Cook the diced bacon in a lightly oiled pan over medium heat until slightly browned. Keep about 2 tbsp of the fat.
  2. Turn the heat to high, add the onion and spring onion, then the chopped kimchi, carrot, 3 tbsp kimchi juice if you have it, soy sauce and gochujang. Stir-fry 3–4 minutes until the kimchi softens.
  3. Add the rice, lower the heat to medium-low and stir 3–4 minutes, breaking up any clumps.
  4. Turn the heat back to high and let the rice toast, turning now and then. Season, then stir in the sesame oil.
  5. Serve topped with a fried egg, strips of nori and sesame seeds.
