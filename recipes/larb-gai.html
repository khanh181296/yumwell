🥗 Larb Gai (Thai Chicken Salad)

Warm minced chicken tossed with lime, fish sauce, fresh herbs and nutty toasted rice. Eat it in lettuce cups.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Ground chicken450 g
  • Sticky rice1 tbsp
  • Cooking oil1 tsp
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Ginger10 g
  • Dried chilies1 tsp
  • Onion1
  • Bell pepper1
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Fish sauce2.5 tbsp
  • Lime2
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Lettuce1 bunch

Steps

  1. Toasted rice: dry-roast the uncooked sticky rice in a pan over medium heat, stirring, until deep golden, then grind it to a coarse powder.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over medium heat and fry the minced garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chicken and chili flakes, breaking the meat up. Cook, stirring often and adding a splash of water if dry, until no pink remains.
  4. Take the wok off the heat. Mix the fish sauce, lime juice and sugar, then toss with the chicken, thinly sliced red onion, chopped pepper, mint, coriander and the toasted rice.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature with lettuce leaves for wrapping, and rice.
