🥩 Bulgogi

Thin beef in a sweet soy, garlic and pear marinade, seared until caramelized.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Beef900 g
  • Spring onion3 stalks
  • Onion1
  • Carrot1
  • Soy sauce6 tbsp
  • Sugar4 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Sesame oil2 tbsp
  • Sesame seeds2 tsp
  • Asian pear0.5
  • Black pepper0.13 tsp

Steps

  1. Separate the thinly sliced beef and blot it dry with paper towels.
  2. Marinade: mix the soy sauce, 3 tbsp water, sugar, mirin, minced garlic, sesame oil, sesame seeds, grated pear and pepper.
  3. Toss the beef, sliced onion, carrot and spring onion with the marinade. Rest 30–60 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
  4. Heat a pan or grill until very hot and cook the beef in small batches so it caramelizes instead of stewing. Serve with rice and lettuce leaves.
