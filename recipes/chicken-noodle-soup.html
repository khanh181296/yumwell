🍜 Chicken Noodle Soup

A warming pot of chicken, carrot, celery and egg noodles in herby broth. The classic soup for chilly days.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 45 min🍽️ Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • Chicken450 g
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Onion1
  • Carrot3
  • Celery2 stalks
  • Black pepper1 tsp
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Thyme1 tbsp
  • Chicken stock1,400 ml
  • Egg noodles150 g

Steps

  1. Simmer the boneless chicken in the stock for about 15 minutes until it reaches 74°C inside. Lift it out, shred it and keep the stock.
  2. Cook the egg noodles in a separate pot as the packet says and drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat and cook the minced garlic, chopped onion, sliced carrot and celery for 7–10 minutes until they start to soften.
  4. Stir in the pepper, salt and thyme (add sage and rosemary if you have them), then the chicken and the stock. Bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Add the noodles and simmer for about 5 minutes, then serve.
