🥢 Zaru Soba (Cold Buckwheat Noodles)

Chilled soba noodles with a savory dashi dipping sauce, scallions and wasabi. Light and done in 15 minutes.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Soba noodles180 g
  • Dashi powder200 ml
  • Soy sauce50 ml
  • Mirin50 ml
  • Spring onion2 tbsp
  • Wasabi1 tsp
  • Nori seaweed1

Steps

  1. Heat the dashi, soy sauce and mirin over medium heat. When small bubbles appear, simmer for 15 seconds, turn off the heat and let it cool.
  2. Boil the soba for 4–6 minutes, or as the packet says, stirring for the first 15 seconds so the strands separate.
  3. Drain, rinse well under cold running water and shake off the excess water.
  4. Arrange on plates, top with shredded nori and serve with the dipping sauce, chopped scallions and wasabi.
