🍳 Tamagoyaki (Rolled Omelette)

Layers of lightly sweet egg rolled into a soft, golden log. Perfect for breakfast or a lunch box.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 10 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Egg4
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Soy sauce0.5 tsp
  • Salt0.13 tsp
  • Cooking oil1 tsp

Steps

  1. Beat the eggs gently with the sugar, soy sauce and salt, trying not to whip in air.
  2. Heat a pan (square if you have one) over medium-low heat and wipe it with oil using a paper towel.
  3. Pour in a thin layer of egg. When the top is no longer runny but still moist, roll it up from one end as tightly as you can.
  4. Push the roll to the far end, oil the empty pan, pour in another thin layer, lifting the roll so the egg runs underneath, and roll it back. Repeat until all the egg is used.
  5. Shape the warm omelette in plastic wrap or a bamboo mat for a minute, then slice into 6–8 pieces.
