🥦 Thai Stir-Fried Vegetables with Shrimp

Crisp mixed vegetables and shrimp stir-fried with golden garlic, oyster sauce and fish sauce. On the table in 25 minutes.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Shrimp225 g
  • Broccoli150 g
  • Carrot1
  • Snow peas1 cups
  • Cabbage100 g
  • Fish sauce2 tsp
  • Oyster sauce2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp

Steps

  1. Cut the vegetables into bite-size pieces. Fry the minced garlic in the oil over medium heat until golden.
  2. Turn the heat to high, add the peeled shrimp and cook until pink.
  3. Add the vegetables, starting with the ones that take longest such as carrot and broccoli, and add water a tablespoon at a time (up to 4) to steam them.
  4. When the vegetables are almost tender, add the fish sauce, oyster sauce and sugar, toss and take off the heat quickly so they stay crisp. Serve with jasmine rice.
