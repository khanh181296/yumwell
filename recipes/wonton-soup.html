🥟 Wonton Soup

Silky pork and greens wontons in a clear chicken broth with sesame oil and white pepper.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 1 h 30 min🍽️ Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • Bok choy280 g
  • Ground pork225 g
  • Sesame oil3 tbsp
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Rice cooking wine2 tbsp
  • Wonton wrappers48
  • Chicken stock1,440 ml

Steps

  1. Blanch the greens in boiling water for 60–90 seconds, rinse in cold water, squeeze dry and chop very finely.
  2. Mix the greens with the pork, 2½ tablespoons sesame oil, a pinch of white pepper, the soy sauce, salt and wine until smooth and paste-like.
  3. Put a teaspoon of filling in each wrapper, wet the edges and fold into a triangle, then bring the two corners together and press to seal.
  4. Heat the stock to a simmer and season with the remaining sesame oil, white pepper and salt.
  5. Boil the wontons in a separate pot of water until they float, then cook 1–2 minutes more so the pork is cooked through. Ladle into bowls of hot broth.
