🥥 Thai Green Curry

Fragrant coconut curry with chicken, eggplant and Thai basil, built on a jar of good paste.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Green curry paste5 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Ginger10 g
  • Lemongrass1 stalks
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Chicken stock250 ml
  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Fish sauce1 tsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Kaffir lime leaves6
  • Chicken350 g
  • Eggplant2
  • Snow peas1.5 cups
  • Holy basil16
  • Lime0.5
  • Rice (cooked)4 cups

Steps

  1. Heat the oil over medium-high. Fry the curry paste with the minced garlic, grated ginger and finely chopped lemongrass for 2–3 minutes until it mostly dries out.
  2. Stir in the stock and coconut milk, then the fish sauce, sugar and torn lime leaves. Bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the sliced chicken thigh, lower to medium so it bubbles gently, and cook 7 minutes.
  4. Add the eggplant slices and cook 5 minutes until soft.
  5. Taste and adjust with fish sauce or sugar. Add the snow peas for 2 minutes, then stir in the basil and lime juice. Serve over jasmine rice. The sauce should be thin, not thick.
