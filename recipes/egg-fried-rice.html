🍳 Egg Fried Rice

Fifteen-minute fried rice with egg, vegetables and a savory sauce. Best with day-old rice.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Rice (cooked)2 cups
  • Egg3
  • Onion0.5
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Bacon120 g
  • Mixed vegetables (peas, carrot, corn)1 cups
  • Spring onion3 stalks
  • Rice cooking wine1 tbsp
  • Oyster sauce1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tsp
  • Black pepper0.25 tsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp

Steps

  1. Use cooked rice that has been chilled (2 cups packed). Chop the onion, garlic and bacon.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok on high until smoking. Fry the onion, garlic and bacon until the bacon is lightly golden.
  3. Add the vegetables and cook until any water evaporates.
  4. Add the rice with the cooking wine, oyster sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil and pepper. Toss until the liquid is absorbed.
  5. Push the rice to one side, add a little oil and scramble the beaten eggs in the gap. Mix in with the spring onion and serve right away.
