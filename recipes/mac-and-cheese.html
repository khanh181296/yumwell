🧀 One-Pot Mac and Cheese

Creamy, cheesy macaroni cooked right in its sauce in one pot. Ready in under 20 minutes.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 18 min🍽️ Serves: 5

Ingredients

  • Butter50 g
  • Flour3 tbsp
  • Milk480 ml
  • Elbow macaroni250 g
  • Mozzarella0.75 cups
  • Cheddar cheese1.5 cups
  • Saltto taste
  • Black pepperto taste

Steps

  1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat, add the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  2. Whisk in about 180 ml of the milk until it turns into a smooth paste, then the rest of the milk and 600 ml water.
  3. Add the macaroni and stir now and then as it heats. When it starts to steam, lower the heat to medium-low.
  4. Stir regularly for 9–10 minutes until the macaroni is just tender and the sauce has thickened but is still saucy.
  5. Take off the heat, quickly stir in both cheeses and season with salt and pepper. Serve straight away while it is creamy.
