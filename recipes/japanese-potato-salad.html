🥔 Japanese Potato Salad

Creamy, half-mashed potatoes with cucumber, carrot and egg in a mayonnaise dressing. A favorite side dish in Japan.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Potato3
  • Egg2
  • Cucumber1
  • Carrot0.5
  • Mayonnaise4 tbsp
  • Vinegar1 tsp
  • Salt0.75 tsp
  • Black pepperto taste

Steps

  1. Peel the potatoes, cut into chunks and boil until very soft. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes until hard-cooked, then cool in cold water.
  2. Thinly slice the cucumber, sprinkle with ¼ teaspoon salt and squeeze out the water after 5 minutes. Cut the carrot into thin quarter slices and boil for 2 minutes.
  3. Drain the potatoes, mash them roughly while hot with the vinegar and the rest of the salt, and let them cool.
  4. Fold in the cucumber, carrot, chopped eggs and mayonnaise. Season with pepper. Keep in the fridge and eat within 2 days.
