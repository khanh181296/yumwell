🍳 Classic Cheese Omelette

Soft, buttery folded omelette filled with cheddar, cherry tomatoes and chives. Breakfast in 10 minutes.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 10 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Egg6
  • Butter60 g
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Cheddar cheese40 g
  • Tomato1
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of black pepper and drop in 10 g of the butter without stirring it in.
  2. Melt the rest of the butter with the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and pour in the eggs once it foams.
  3. Stir quickly with a spatula, moving the pan on and off the heat, until the eggs look like soft scrambled eggs. Lower the heat, spread them evenly and cook until set.
  4. Season with salt, scatter the grated cheddar and halved cherry tomatoes down the middle, loosen the edges and fold both sides over.
  5. Slide onto a plate and sprinkle with chopped chives.
