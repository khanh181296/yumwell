🌱 Korean Bean Sprout Soup

A clear, light soup of bean sprouts, garlic and scallions. Comforting, vegetarian and ready in 20 minutes.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Bean sprouts300 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Korean chili flakes0.5 tsp

Steps

  1. Rinse the bean sprouts and pick off any brown roots. Mince the garlic and slice the scallions.
  2. Put the sprouts in a pot with 1.2 liters of water, cover and bring to a boil. Keep the lid on and simmer so the sprouts stay sweet and crunchy.
  3. Add the garlic, soy sauce and salt and simmer for 2 more minutes, uncovered.
  4. Finish with the scallions, and the chili flakes if you like a little heat.
