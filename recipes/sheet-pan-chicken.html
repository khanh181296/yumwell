🍗 Sheet-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken

Chicken thighs roasted on one tray with potatoes, carrots and onion, flavored with lemon, garlic and thyme.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 55 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Chicken1,000 g
  • Potato3
  • Carrot2
  • Onion1
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Lemon1
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Thyme1 tsp
  • Salt1.5 tsp
  • Black pepper0.5 tsp

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut the potatoes and carrots into chunks and the onion into wedges.
  2. Toss the vegetables and the bone-in chicken thighs with the olive oil, smashed garlic, thyme, salt, pepper and the juice of half the lemon. Spread in one layer on a large tray, chicken skin side up.
  3. Roast until the skin is golden, the potatoes are tender and the chicken reaches 74°C at the thickest part near the bone.
  4. Squeeze over the rest of the lemon and serve straight from the tray.
