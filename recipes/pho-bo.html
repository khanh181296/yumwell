🍜 Beef Pho

Slow-simmered beef broth with charred ginger and warm spices, rice noodles and fresh herbs.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 3 h 30 min🍽️ Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • Beef1,500 g
  • Beef bones2,000 g
  • Onion2
  • Ginger150 g
  • Star anise10
  • Cinnamon stick4
  • Coriander seeds1.5 tbsp
  • Fish sauce3 tbsp
  • Sugar2 tbsp
  • Salt1 tbsp
  • Flat rice noodles300 g
  • Beef tenderloin200 g
  • Bean sprouts200 g
  • Holy basil1 bunch
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Lime2
  • Chili1

Steps

  1. Halve the onions and slice the ginger. Char them cut side down in a very hot dry pan until blackened in spots. Toast the star anise, cinnamon and coriander seeds in the same pan for about 3 minutes.
  2. Cover the bones and brisket with water, boil hard for 5 minutes, drain and rinse each piece. This keeps the broth clear.
  3. Return the bones and brisket to a clean pot with 3.5 liters of water, the charred aromatics, spices, sugar and salt. Cover and simmer very gently.
  4. Lift out the brisket and let it cool, then slice. Simmer the broth uncovered to reduce to about 2.5 liters.
  5. Strain the broth, add the fish sauce and adjust the salt and sugar.
  6. Slice the tenderloin paper-thin (15 minutes in the freezer makes this easier). Cook the noodles as the packet says.
  7. Put noodles, brisket and raw beef in bowls and ladle the boiling broth over to cook the beef. Serve with bean sprouts, basil, herbs, lime and chili.
