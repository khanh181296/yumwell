🍅 Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

The ultimate Chinese home dish: fluffy eggs in a sweet-savory tomato sauce. Ready in 10 minutes.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 10 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Tomato500 g
  • Egg4
  • Spring onion1 stalks
  • Salt0.75 tsp
  • Black pepper0.25 tsp
  • Sesame oil0.5 tsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tsp
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Cooking oil3 tbsp

Steps

  1. Cut the tomatoes into small wedges and chop the spring onion. Beat the eggs for a minute with 1/4 tsp salt, the pepper, sesame oil and cooking wine.
  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil until just smoking, scramble the eggs until barely set and take them out.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil, turn the heat to high and stir-fry the tomatoes and spring onion for 1 minute.
  4. Add the sugar, remaining salt and 60 ml water. Return the eggs, cover and cook 1–2 minutes until the tomatoes soften. Uncover and reduce the sauce to your liking. Serve with rice.
