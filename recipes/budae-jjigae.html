🍜 Army Stew (Budae Jjigae)

A spicy kimchi stew loaded with sausages, ham, tofu and ramen noodles, made for sharing.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Kimchi2 cups
  • Hot dogs or sausages2
  • Bacon3 slices
  • Luncheon meat115 g
  • Tofu115 g
  • Onion0.5
  • Mushrooms60 g
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Bell pepper0.25
  • Chicken stock960 ml
  • Korean chili flakes1 tbsp
  • Gochujang1 tsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Instant ramen noodles1

Steps

  1. Cut the kimchi, sausages, bacon, luncheon meat, tofu, onion, mushrooms, scallions and pepper into bite-size pieces and arrange them in a wide shallow pot.
  2. Mix the chili flakes, gochujang, soy sauce, minced garlic, black pepper and 1–2 tablespoons stock into a paste.
  3. Pour in the stock, add the seasoning paste and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook until the bacon is cooked through and the kimchi is soft.
  4. Briefly boil the ramen noodles in a separate pot, add them to the stew with more stock if needed, and keep it bubbling at the table.
