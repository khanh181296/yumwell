🥞 Scallion Pancakes

Flaky, chewy pan-fried flatbreads layered with scallions, a favorite Chinese breakfast and snack.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 45 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Flour300 g
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Spring onion6 stalks
  • Cooking oil6 tbsp
  • Five spice powder0.25 tsp

Steps

  1. Stir 120 ml of boiling water into the flour with chopsticks, then add about 80 ml of cold water a little at a time to make a soft dough. Knead for 5 minutes until smooth, cover and rest.
  2. Mix 2 tablespoons of oil with the salt and five-spice. Finely chop the scallions.
  3. Divide the dough into 4. Roll each piece into a thin rectangle, brush with the spiced oil and scatter with scallions. Roll it up like a rope, coil it into a snail, tuck the end under and gently roll it flat to about 5 mm.
  4. Fry each pancake in 1 tablespoon of oil over medium heat for about 3 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Scrunch it with two spatulas to open the layers, then cut into wedges.
