🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

Light, fluffy breakfast pancakes from pantry staples in 20 minutes.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Flour2 cups
  • Baking powder4 tsp
  • Sugar55 g
  • Salt1 pinch
  • Egg1
  • Milk435 ml
  • Vanilla extract1 tsp
  • Butter20 g
  • Honeyto taste

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg, milk and vanilla and whisk until just smooth, no more than 30 seconds.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium. Melt a little butter and wipe most of it off with paper towel.
  4. Pour 60 ml of batter into the pan. When bubbles appear (about 30 seconds), flip and cook 30–45 seconds more until golden.
  5. Repeat with the rest, adding butter every 2–3 pancakes. Serve warm with honey or fruit.
