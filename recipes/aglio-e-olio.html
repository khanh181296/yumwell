🧄 Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Spaghetti tossed in golden garlic olive oil with chili flakes and parsley. Pantry dinner in under 20 minutes.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 18 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti500 g
  • Salt3 tsp
  • Olive oil120 ml
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Dried chilies0.5 tsp
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Parmesan30 g

Steps

  1. Bring 4 liters of water to a boil with 2 teaspoons salt and cook the spaghetti for 1 minute less than the packet says. Scoop out a cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. About 4 minutes before the pasta is ready, warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and gently fry the thinly sliced garlic for 2–3 minutes until very light golden. Add the chili flakes for 20–30 seconds. Do not let the garlic brown or it turns bitter.
  3. Add the drained pasta, 125 ml pasta water and 1 teaspoon salt. Toss over medium-high heat for 1–1½ minutes until the oil and water turn into a sauce that clings to the pasta; add more water if it gets sticky.
  4. Toss in the chopped parsley and serve straight away with grated Parmesan.
