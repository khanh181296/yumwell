🥜 Kung Pao Chicken

Sichuan stir-fry of tender chicken, crunchy peanuts, dried chilies and a glossy sweet-sour sauce.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Chicken350 g
  • Peanuts1 cups
  • Cornstarch2 tsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Dark soy sauce0.5 tsp
  • Vinegar1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Ginger5 g
  • Dried chilies4
  • Sichuan pepper0.5 tsp
  • Spring onion6 stalks
  • Cooking oil3 tbsp

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into 2 cm cubes and toss with 1 tsp oil, 1 tsp cornstarch, the cooking wine, a pinch of salt and pepper. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. If your peanuts are raw, stir them in a little oil over medium heat for 3 minutes, then let them cool until crunchy.
  3. Sauce: mix both soy sauces, vinegar, sugar, 3 tbsp water and 1 tsp cornstarch.
  4. Sear the chicken in 2 tbsp oil in a very hot wok until golden, then take it out.
  5. Turn the heat to low. Add 1 tbsp oil, the sliced garlic, minced ginger, chopped dried chilies, Sichuan pepper and the white parts of the spring onion. Stir 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Return the chicken, turn the heat to high and stir for 1 minute. Stir the sauce again, pour it in and cook until glossy, about 1 minute. Toss in the peanuts and serve with rice.
