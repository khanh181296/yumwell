🥬 Garlic Water Spinach (Rau Muống Xào Tỏi)

Crisp water spinach flash-fried with lots of garlic and a glossy oyster sauce. Ready in 15 minutes.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Water spinach900 g
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Oyster sauce3 tbsp
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Cornstarch2 tsp

Steps

  1. Trim the tough ends and break the water spinach into 12 cm pieces, keeping stems and leaves apart. Wash well in a big bowl of water, lifting the greens out so the grit stays behind.
  2. Mix the oyster sauce, sugar and cornstarch in a small bowl.
  3. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Fry the minced garlic for a few seconds, add the stems and stir-fry for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the leaves and 2 tablespoons water, cover and steam for 2 minutes. Do not overcook or it turns dull and slimy.
  5. Pour in the sauce, toss until coated and serve at once.
