🫒 Greek Salad

Crunchy tomato, cucumber, pepper, olives and feta with an oregano olive oil dressing.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Tomato3
  • Cucumber4
  • Onion0.5
  • Bell pepper1
  • Feta cheese250 g
  • Olives125 g
  • Dried oregano2 tsp
  • Garlic1 cloves
  • Vinegar3 tbsp
  • Olive oil6 tbsp
  • Salt0.25 tsp
  • Black pepperto taste

Steps

  1. Dressing: shake the minced garlic, 1 tsp oregano, salt, pepper, vinegar and olive oil in a jar. Let it sit 20 minutes.
  2. Cut the tomatoes into chunks, slice the cucumbers 5 mm thick, slice the red onion into thin rings and the pepper into short strips. Cube the feta (1 cm).
  3. Combine everything with the olives, sprinkle with the remaining oregano, pour over the dressing and toss. Serve right away.
