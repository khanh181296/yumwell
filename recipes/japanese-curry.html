🍛 Japanese Chicken Curry

Mild, thick and cozy curry with chicken, potato and carrot, made easy with curry roux.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Chicken400 g
  • Onion400 g
  • Potato250 g
  • Carrot100 g
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Japanese curry roux115 g
  • Rice (cooked)4 cups

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces, slice the onion 1 cm wide, cube the potato (1.5 cm) and slice the carrot.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium-high. Brown the chicken, then sauté the onion until translucent and starting to color.
  3. Add the potato and carrot and stir for a couple of minutes.
  4. Add 800 ml water, bring to a boil, then simmer on medium-low about 7 minutes until the vegetables are nearly tender.
  5. Break in the curry roux, stir gently, then cover and cook on low about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until fully dissolved and thick. Add water if too thick (check your roux pack for the right ratio).
  6. Serve beside hot rice.
