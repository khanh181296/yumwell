🍱 Katsudon (Pork Cutlet Rice Bowl)

Crispy pork cutlet and onion simmered in sweet dashi and soy, bound with egg and served over rice.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Pork chop400 g
  • Saltto taste
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Flour1.5 tbsp
  • Egg3
  • Panko breadcrumbs40 g
  • Cooking oil500 ml
  • Dashi powder100 ml
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tbsp
  • Onion0.5
  • Rice (cooked)2 cups

Steps

  1. Make tonkatsu: cut through the connective tissue of two pork loin steaks every 2–3 cm, pound lightly and season. Coat in flour, one beaten egg and panko.
  2. Fry at 170°C for 2 minutes, turn and fry 2 minutes, then turn again for 1 minute until golden and cooked through. Drain and cut into 2 cm strips.
  3. Mix the dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sake. In a small pan, bring half the sauce and half the sliced onion to a boil over medium-high heat.
  4. Lower to medium, lay one cutlet in the middle and simmer for 2 minutes. Pour a beaten egg over the top, cover and cook until the egg is set to your liking.
  5. Slide it onto a bowl of hot rice and repeat for the second bowl.
