🥦 Beef and Broccoli

Takeout-style tender beef and broccoli in a glossy savory sauce, done in 20 minutes.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Beef360 g
  • Broccoli1
  • Cornstarch2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Dark soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1.5 tbsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tsp
  • Black pepper0.13 tsp
  • Garlic1 cloves
  • Ginger5 g
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Sesame seedsto taste

Steps

  1. Sauce: mix the cornstarch with 60 ml water, then add the sugar, both soy sauces, cooking wine, sesame oil and pepper.
  2. Slice the beef 5 mm thick against the grain and mix it with 2 tbsp of the sauce.
  3. Cut the broccoli into florets and boil for 40–60 seconds, then drain.
  4. Heat the oil on high. Spread the beef out and leave it for 1 minute to brown, stir, then add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  5. Pour in the sauce and 240 ml water. When it bubbles, add the broccoli and simmer about 1 minute until glossy. Top with sesame seeds and serve with rice.
