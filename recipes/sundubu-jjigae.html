🍲 Soft Tofu Stew (Sundubu Jjigae)

Silky soft tofu in a bubbling spicy broth with kimchi and thin slices of pork. Under 30 minutes.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 25 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Tofu340 g
  • Kimchi0.33 cups
  • Pork85 g
  • Korean chili flakes2 tsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Sesame oil1 tbsp
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Spring onion1 stalks
  • Egg1

Steps

  1. Cut the pork and kimchi into thin strips. Put them in a small pot with the chili flakes, minced garlic and sesame oil over medium heat.
  2. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until the pork is almost cooked.
  3. Add 240 ml water and 2–3 tablespoons kimchi juice. Boil for 3–4 minutes, skimming off any foam.
  4. Spoon in the tofu in large chunks, season with salt (start with ¼ teaspoon) and black pepper, and cook for 4–5 minutes.
  5. Add the scallion. Crack in the egg and let it cook in the bubbling stew until set before serving with rice.
