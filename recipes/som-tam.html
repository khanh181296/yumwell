🥗 Som Tam (Green Papaya Salad)

Crunchy shredded green papaya with tomato, green beans and peanuts in a sour, salty, spicy lime dressing.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 10 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Lime2
  • Fish sauce3 tbsp
  • Garlic1 cloves
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Chili2
  • Green papaya500 g
  • Tomato150 g
  • Green beans80 g
  • Peanuts50 g
  • Dried shrimp15 g
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Dressing: blend or whisk 80 ml lime juice with the fish sauce, chilies, grated garlic and sugar until smooth.
  2. Peel and halve the papaya, scoop out the seeds and white pith, and shred it into a large bowl.
  3. Lightly pound the 4 cm lengths of green bean and the dried shrimp to tenderize them.
  4. Add the beans, shrimp, tomato pieces, crushed peanuts and coriander to the papaya, pour over the dressing and toss well. Serve straight away.
