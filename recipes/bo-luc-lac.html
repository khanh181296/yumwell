🥩 Shaking Beef (Bò Lúc Lắc)

Seared cubes of beef glazed with oyster sauce, tossed with onion and garlic over a fresh salad.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Beef tenderloin600 g
  • Oyster sauce4 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Black pepper1 tsp
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Onion1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Lettuce1 bunch
  • Tomato2
  • Cucumber1
  • Lime1

Steps

  1. Mix the oyster sauce, sesame oil, sugar, salt and pepper. Cut the beef into 2 cm cubes, toss them in the marinade and leave for 30 minutes.
  2. Thickly slice the red onion and chop the garlic. Arrange the lettuce, tomato and cucumber slices on a platter.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over very high heat. Sear the beef in batches, less than 1 minute per side, so it browns instead of stewing. Set aside.
  4. Fry the onion and garlic for about 1 minute without burning the garlic. Return the beef and its juices and toss until glossy.
  5. Spoon the beef and pan juices over the salad. Serve with a dip of lime juice, salt and pepper, and rice if you like.
