🍜 Yakisoba

Japanese stir-fried noodles with pork, cabbage and a tangy-sweet yakisoba sauce.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Egg noodles450 g
  • Pork belly340 g
  • Cabbage230 g
  • Mushrooms40 g
  • Carrot1
  • Onion0.5
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Worcestershire sauce4 tbsp
  • Oyster sauce4 tsp
  • Ketchup4 tsp
  • Soy sauce2 tsp
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Black pepperto taste

Steps

  1. Sauce: whisk the Worcestershire, oyster sauce, ketchup, soy sauce and sugar.
  2. Cut the pork into 2.5 cm pieces, slice the onion and mushrooms, julienne the carrot, cut the cabbage and spring onion into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Warm the steamed noodles in the oil on both sides, loosen them gently and set aside.
  4. Stir-fry the pork with pepper until no longer pink. Add the onion 1–2 minutes, the carrot 1–2 minutes, then the cabbage and mushrooms until starting to soften, and the spring onion for 1 minute.
  5. Put the noodles on top, pour over about 80 ml of sauce and toss until coated and hot. Add more sauce to taste and serve right away.
