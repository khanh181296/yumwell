🍝 Spaghetti Bolognese

Rich tomato and beef sauce simmered with red wine and herbs, tossed through spaghetti.

🌏 Western⏱️ Time: 40 min🍽️ Serves: 5

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Ground beef500 g
  • Olive oil1.5 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Onion1
  • Red wine125 ml
  • Beef stock cube2
  • Canned tomatoes800 g
  • Sugar2 tsp
  • Worcestershire sauce2 tsp
  • Bay leaves2
  • Thyme2 stalks
  • Salt0.75 tsp
  • Black pepper0.5 tsp
  • Parmesanto taste

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil over medium-high. Cook the chopped onion and garlic about 3 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  2. Turn the heat to high, add the beef and break it up until browned.
  3. Pour in the red wine (or water) and simmer 1 minute, scraping the bottom of the pot.
  4. Add the crumbled stock cubes, crushed tomatoes, sugar, Worcestershire, bay leaves, thyme, salt and pepper. Simmer uncovered on medium for 20–30 minutes, adding water if it gets too thick.
  5. Cook the spaghetti in salted water for 1 minute less than the packet says. Keep a mug of pasta water.
  6. Toss the spaghetti in the sauce with about 125 ml pasta water for 1½–2 minutes until it clings. Serve with parmesan.
