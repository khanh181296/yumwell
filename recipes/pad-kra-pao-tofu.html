🌶️ Thai Basil Tofu

A vegetarian take on the Thai street favorite: crumbled tofu stir-fried with garlic, chili and basil over rice.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Tofu400 g
  • Garlic5 cloves
  • Chili4
  • Green beans100 g
  • Holy basil1 bunch
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Dark soy sauce1 tsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Cooking oil3 tbsp
  • Rice (cooked)2 cups

Steps

  1. Press the firm tofu dry and crumble it into small pieces. Coarsely pound the garlic and chilies. Cut the green beans into short lengths.
  2. Fry the tofu in 2 tablespoons of oil over high heat, stirring now and then, until golden at the edges. Push it to one side.
  3. Add the rest of the oil and fry the garlic and chilies for 30 seconds, then add the green beans and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Add both soy sauces, the sugar and 2 tablespoons of water and toss everything together. Off the heat, stir in the basil until it wilts. Serve over rice.
