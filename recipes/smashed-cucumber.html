🥒 Smashed Cucumber Salad

Cool, crunchy cucumbers smashed and dressed in garlic, vinegar, soy sauce and chili oil. Ready in ten minutes.

🌏 Chinese⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Cucumber2
  • Salt0.5 tsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Vinegar1.5 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Sesame oil1 tsp
  • Chili1

Steps

  1. Wash the cucumbers well. Lay them on a board and press down with the flat of a knife until they crack, then cut into bite-size pieces.
  2. Toss with the salt and leave for 10 minutes, then pour off the liquid.
  3. Mix the minced garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and sliced chili.
  4. Pour over the cucumbers, toss and serve cold.
