🍗 Thai Chicken and Rice (Khao Man Gai)

Tender poached chicken on fragrant rice cooked in the chicken broth, with a punchy ginger and chili sauce.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 1 h🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Chicken1,000 g
  • Ginger50 g
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Salt2 tsp
  • Uncooked rice400 g
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Dark soy sauce1 tsp
  • Vinegar1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Chili2
  • Cucumber1
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Put the chicken (bone-in thighs work best) in a pot with half the ginger, sliced, 3 smashed garlic cloves, 1½ teaspoons salt and enough water to cover. Bring to a boil, skim, then simmer gently until cooked through to the bone (74°C).
  2. Lift out the chicken, plunge it into cold water for a few minutes so the skin stays smooth, then drain. Keep the broth.
  3. Rinse the rice. Fry 2 minced garlic cloves in the oil until fragrant, add the rice and stir for 2 minutes. Cook it in a rice cooker or pot with chicken broth instead of water and ½ teaspoon salt.
  4. Sauce: mix the remaining ginger and garlic, minced, with the soy sauces, vinegar, sugar, chopped chili and 1 tablespoon of broth.
  5. Slice the chicken and serve on the rice with cucumber, herbs, the sauce and a bowl of hot broth.
