🌶️ Spicy Stir-Fried Chicken (Dakgalbi)

Chicken thigh in a sweet-spicy gochujang sauce, cooked in one pan with cabbage, sweet potato and chewy rice cakes.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 40 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Chicken450 g
  • Cabbage225 g
  • Sweet potato1
  • Rice cakes (tteok)225 g
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Korean chili flakes2 tbsp
  • Sugar1.5 tbsp
  • Curry powder1 tsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Rice cooking wine2 tbsp
  • Gochujang2 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Ginger5 g
  • Sesame oil1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Soak the rice cakes in cold water for about 20 minutes.
  2. Sauce: mix the chili flakes, sugar, curry powder, soy sauce, rice wine, gochujang, minced garlic, grated ginger and sesame oil.
  3. Cut the chicken thighs into bite-size pieces, coat them in the sauce and leave in the fridge while you slice the sweet potato thinly and cut the cabbage, perilla or other herbs and scallion.
  4. Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken, sweet potato, rice cakes and cabbage and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring now and then; add 60 ml water if it gets dry.
  5. Add the herbs and scallion and cook for 3–4 minutes more, until the rice cakes and sweet potato are soft and the chicken is cooked through. Wrap in lettuce if you like.
