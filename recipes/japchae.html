🍝 Japchae (Glass Noodle Stir-Fry)

Chewy sweet potato noodles with beef, spinach and colorful vegetables in a sesame-soy sauce.

🌏 Korean⏱️ Time: 35 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato noodles200 g
  • Beef115 g
  • Carrot1
  • Onion0.5
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Mushrooms100 g
  • Spinach170 g
  • Soy sauce3.5 tbsp
  • Sugar3 tbsp
  • Sesame oil2 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Sesame seeds1 tbsp
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp

Steps

  1. Cut the carrot into matchsticks, slice the onion and mushrooms, cut the spring onion into 5 cm pieces and the beef into thin strips.
  2. Sauce: mix the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, minced garlic, sesame seeds and pepper until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Boil the noodles 6–8 minutes until clear and soft. Rinse in cold water, drain, cut into shorter lengths and toss with 3 tbsp of the sauce.
  4. Blanch the spinach just until wilted, rinse in cold water, squeeze dry and season with a pinch of salt.
  5. Stir-fry the noodles in ½ tbsp oil for about 4 minutes until glossy, then put them in a big bowl.
  6. In ½ tbsp oil, stir-fry the carrot 1 minute, then the onion until clear, then the spring onion briefly, salting lightly. Add to the bowl.
  7. Cook the beef and mushrooms with 2 tbsp sauce for 2–3 minutes. Add everything to the bowl with the spinach and remaining sauce and toss well.
