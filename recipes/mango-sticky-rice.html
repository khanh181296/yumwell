🥭 Mango Sticky Rice

Steamed sticky rice soaked in sweet coconut cream, served with ripe mango.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 45 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Sticky rice1 cups
  • Coconut milk420 ml
  • Sugar3 tbsp
  • Salt1 pinch
  • Cornstarch1 tbsp
  • Mango1
  • Sesame seeds1 tsp

Steps

  1. Rinse the sticky rice twice, then soak it in water in the fridge for at least 4 hours or overnight. Drain.
  2. Steam the rice on a damp cloth in a steamer over boiling water for about 20 minutes, until translucent and tender but still chewy.
  3. Bring the coconut milk to a boil, lower the heat and stir in the sugar and salt until dissolved. Whisk in the cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water and let it thicken for 1 minute.
  4. Pour half the sauce into a bowl. Stir the hot rice into the rest, cover and let it soak up the liquid for 15 minutes.
  5. Serve the rice with mango slices, a spoon of the reserved sauce and sesame seeds.
