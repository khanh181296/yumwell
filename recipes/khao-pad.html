🍤 Thai Shrimp Fried Rice (Khao Pad)

Jasmine rice fried with shrimp, egg and chili in a fish sauce seasoning, served with cucumber and lime.

🌏 Thai⏱️ Time: 15 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Rice (cooked)3 cups
  • Shrimp250 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Shallot1
  • Chili3
  • Spring onion3 stalks
  • Egg2
  • Cooking oil3 tbsp
  • Fish sauce1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tbsp
  • Cucumber1
  • Lime1
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch

Steps

  1. Use cold cooked jasmine rice from the fridge. Stir the fish sauce, soy sauce and sugar together until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a wok over medium heat. Fry the chopped shallot and garlic for a few seconds, add the peeled shrimp and cook for 1–2 minutes until pink.
  3. Push the shrimp aside, add the remaining oil and the eggs, let them set briefly, then scramble. Add the sliced chilies.
  4. Add the rice and the sauce, turn up the heat and stir-fry for about 2 minutes until everything is steaming hot.
  5. Toss in the scallions for a minute and serve with cucumber slices, lime wedges and coriander.
