🫚 Ginger Pork (Shogayaki)

Thin slices of pork seared and glazed in a punchy ginger, soy and mirin sauce. On the table in 20 minutes.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 20 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Pork300 g
  • Rice cooking wine2.5 tbsp
  • Ginger25 g
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Mirin1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Cabbage160 g
  • Tomato1

Steps

  1. Use pork sliced 2–3 mm thick. Coat it with 2 teaspoons sake and 1 teaspoon ginger juice squeezed from grated ginger, and leave for 10 minutes.
  2. Sauce: mix 2 tablespoons sake, the mirin, soy sauce, sugar and 1 tablespoon grated ginger.
  3. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Lay the pork in a single layer (in batches) and cook for 1–2 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  4. Turn the heat to medium, return all the pork, pour in the sauce and turn the slices for 15–30 seconds until the sauce is reduced and glossy.
  5. Serve on a bed of shredded cabbage with tomato, pouring the pan sauce over the top.
