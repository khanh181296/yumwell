🎃 Pumpkin and Mushroom Soup

A light, naturally sweet vegetarian soup of pumpkin and mushrooms, ready in half an hour.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Pumpkin500 g
  • Mushrooms150 g
  • Shallot2
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Sugar0.5 tsp
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro)1 bunch
  • Black pepperto taste

Steps

  1. Peel and seed the pumpkin and cut it into 3 cm chunks. Slice the mushrooms and the shallots.
  2. Sauté the shallots in the oil until fragrant, then add the mushrooms and stir for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the pumpkin and 1.2 liters of water. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the pumpkin is tender.
  4. Season with the salt, sugar and soy sauce. Finish with sliced scallions, herbs and pepper.
