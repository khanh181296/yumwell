🥞 Okonomiyaki (Savory Cabbage Pancake)

A thick cabbage pancake topped with pork and egg, finished with tangy sauce, mayo and bonito flakes.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 45 min🍽️ Serves: 3

Ingredients

  • Flour1.5 cups
  • Dashi powder350 ml
  • Egg3
  • Cabbage210 g
  • Pork belly120 g
  • Bonito flakes (katsuobushi)0.5 cups
  • Cooking oil2 tbsp
  • Tonkatsu sauce3 tbsp
  • Mayonnaise2 tbsp

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour and dashi until smooth, then mix in 140 g shredded cabbage and one egg.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large pan over medium heat. Pour in a little less than half the batter and spread it into an 18 cm round.
  3. Scatter half the remaining cabbage on top, make a well and crack in an egg, then lay half the thinly sliced pork and sprinkle half the bonito flakes. Drizzle a little of the reserved batter over the top.
  4. Cook for about 10 minutes until the bottom is golden and the pancake holds together, flip and cook 10 minutes more until the pork is cooked through and a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Flip it topping-side up, drizzle with tonkatsu sauce and mayonnaise, and cut into quarters. Repeat with the rest.
