🍗 Karaage (Japanese Fried Chicken)

Juicy chicken thigh marinated in soy, sake and ginger, coated in cornstarch and fried twice for extra crunch.

🌏 Japanese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Chicken350 g
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Rice cooking wine1 tbsp
  • Mirin0.5 tsp
  • Ginger10 g
  • Cornstarch20 g
  • Cooking oil500 ml
  • Cabbage80 g

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken thighs into large bite-size pieces and pat dry. Massage with the soy sauce, sake, mirin and grated ginger with its juice, and marinate for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
  2. Heat 3–4 cm of oil to 160°C. Drain the chicken, pat dry and toss in the cornstarch.
  3. Fry in batches for 2½–3 minutes, then rest on paper towels for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Skim the oil, heat it to 190–200°C and fry the chicken again for 30 seconds to 1 minute until golden, crunchy and cooked through.
  5. Serve straight away with shredded cabbage.
