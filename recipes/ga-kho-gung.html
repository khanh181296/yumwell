🍗 Ginger Caramel Chicken

Bite-sized chicken braised in caramel, fish sauce and plenty of ginger.

🌏 Vietnamese⏱️ Time: 30 min🍽️ Serves: 5

Ingredients

  • Chicken1,300 g
  • Fish sauce3 tbsp
  • Sugar4 tbsp
  • Black pepper0.5 tsp
  • Ginger70 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Shallot2
  • Cooking oil1 tbsp
  • Spring onion2 stalks
  • Chili1

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into 4 cm pieces. Marinate with the fish sauce, 2 tbsp sugar and the pepper for at least 30 minutes (overnight is even better).
  2. Cut the ginger into thin matchsticks, mince the garlic and shallots.
  3. In a wide lidded pan, heat the oil with the remaining 2 tbsp sugar over medium-high until it turns dark amber. Watch closely so it does not burn.
  4. Fry the ginger, garlic and shallots for 30 seconds, add the chicken and toss until lightly browned.
  5. Pour in 240 ml water (or coconut water), cover and simmer on low for 10 minutes. Toss, then cook uncovered 10 minutes more until the sauce is sticky. Season to taste.
  6. Finish with black pepper, spring onion and chili. Serve with rice and a vegetable.
